- (direction) Use the financing-scenarios POC brief, updated domain context, and ADR-004 as the source contract; prioritize a fast production POC followed by incremental complexity.
- (decision) Use express distillation because the source materials fully define the POC boundary and primary outcomes; do not run interactive elicitation or story breakdown.
- (decision) Use stable capability namespace CAP-1 through CAP-7 for this new financing-scenarios spec.
- (capability) CAP-1: Users can save a hypothetical Financing Scenario and compare it without changing recorded assets, liabilities, transactions, or PortfolioSnapshots; success is a before-and-after portfolio integrity test showing no mutation.
- (capability) CAP-2: Users can compare Cash Financing, Custom Down Payment Financing, and Full Financing for one Purchase Price and Financing Horizon; success is a result set containing all three options with consistent shared inputs.
- (capability) CAP-3: Users can confirm per-asset financing availability and enter a constant Savings Return Assumption for each eligible savings asset; success is that eligible balances and rates affect projections while unavailable balances and rates do not.
- (capability) CAP-4: The system can project each option month by month using proportional withdrawal, per-asset monthly returns, fixed monthly availability, and Standard Loan amortisation; success is the reference calculation reproducing its published monthly and one-year values.
- (capability) CAP-5: Users can inspect yearly checkpoints and monthly timeline detail for the Financing Horizon; success is a 60-month scenario exposing yearly summaries plus every monthly payment, return, savings balance, loan balance, and breach timing.
- (capability) CAP-6: Users can compare total Financing Cost and Savings Return retained or lost against Cash Financing; success is a signed return difference and a separate financing-cost value for every option, with Purchase Price excluded from Financing Cost.
- (capability) CAP-7: Users can see Final Scenario Savings Balance and the first Reserve Breach month without being blocked from comparing options; success is a visible warning whenever the balance first falls below Emergency Reserve and no validation failure solely because of that breach.
- (constraint) The comparison is hypothetical and must remain separate from accounting records; no scenario calculation may create or mutate assets, liabilities, transactions, or PortfolioSnapshots.
- (constraint) The same Purchase Price and Financing Horizon must be used across all options so the comparison remains meaningful; the first version uses the Standard Loan duration as the horizon.
- (constraint) Availability is an explicit user-confirmed scenario input; asset type names must not decide legal, tax, or product availability.
- (constraint) Blocked or unavailable assets are excluded from Starting Savings, never reduced by outlays or monthly withdrawals, and do not contribute returns to the comparison.
- (constraint) Eligible asset balances retain their own Savings Return Assumption; the POC must not collapse distinct products into one rate.
- (constraint) Withdrawal allocation is deterministic proportional withdrawal by current eligible balance and must be visible; the POC must not silently optimise account selection.
- (constraint) Monthly Available Amount is fixed for the full scenario; salary growth and changing monthly availability are outside the POC.
- (constraint) Savings return is applied monthly and loan repayment is amortised monthly into interest and principal; yearly checkpoints are presentation summaries, not a lower-fidelity calculation path.
- (constraint) Reserve Breach is a warning and identifies the first month below Emergency Reserve; it is never a hard comparison blocker.
- (constraint) Cheapest Financing is only the lowest Financing Cost, defined as loan interest plus Loan Setup Fee; it is not a universal recommendation and must remain separate from return and final-balance outputs.
- (constraint) Existing savings returns common to all options are not treated as a financing benefit; the comparison uses the difference created by each option versus Cash Financing.
- (constraint) The result must expose material assumptions: eligible assets, each asset's return, availability, withdrawal rule, Monthly Available Amount, Emergency Reserve, loan terms, fees, Purchase Price, and Financing Horizon.
- (direction) Keep Terminal Residual Value, overall Scenario Position, refinancing, taxes, inflation, investment risk, ownership costs, salary growth, changing availability, automatic residual estimates, early repayment, multiple lenders, and apply-scenario mutation outside the POC.
- (assumption) Treat the financing-scenarios POC brief as fully absorbed into SPEC.md and the calculation-model.md companion; downstream consumers do not need to reread its narrative source.
- (assumption) Treat ADR-004 and CONTEXT.md as adopted companions because their accepted planning/accounting boundary and domain vocabulary remain useful to downstream implementation.

- (question) When exactly within a month are savings returns, loan interest, loan payment, and the net Monthly Available Amount applied for cases beyond the reference example?
