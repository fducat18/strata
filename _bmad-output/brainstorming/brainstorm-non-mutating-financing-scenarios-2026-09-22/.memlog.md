- (direction by user) Use CONTEXT.md and ADR-004 as inputs; prioritize a fast production POC followed by incremental complexity.
- (technique by coach) Started Facilitator Chosen flow: Job to Be Done, One Feature Only, Disney Method, Failure Analysis.
- (idea by user) The user wants to compare financing options before a purchase, choose the cheapest option, preserve a minimum vital saving, and consider borrowing when savings returns could cover borrowing costs.
- (insight by coach) The job combines cost optimization with liquidity safety; the POC may need to distinguish cheapest financing cost from best end position and reserve protection.
- (direction by user) For the POC, cheapest means the financing criterion: money spent on financing, especially interest and setup fees. Monthly available amount is treated as roughly constant; salary growth and external changes are out of scope.
- (insight by coach) POC should not optimize optional liquidity or salary growth; it should compare financing cost directly, while preserving emergency reserve as a safety constraint. Savings return is needed only to test when retained savings can offset borrowing cost.
- (direction by user) Savings Return Assumption is mandatory in the POC because Strata has the user’s Starting Savings and a small return on a large retained balance can materially affect the comparison.
- (insight by coach) Correction: ignoring savings return only proves cash has lower financing cost in the narrow sense; it does not prove cash has the best projected position. The model must apply savings returns to each option’s Scenario Savings Balance and compare the incremental retained amount against borrowing cost.
- (direction by user) Create a dedicated POC scope file containing the savings-return comparison example; a Reserve Breach is allowed and shown as a warning, not a blocker.
- (decision by coach) POC scope captured in docs/src/content/docs/features/financing-scenarios-poc.md with separate Cheapest Financing and Projected Position outputs, mandatory savings return, fixed monthly availability, and non-blocking reserve warnings.
- (technique by coach) Switched to One Feature Only: force the POC to have one capability that is exceptionally trustworthy.
- (idea by user) The POC must compare Cash, Full Loan, and mixed cash-plus-loan financing, and identify total loan cost alongside savings returns kept versus returns lost when savings fund the purchase.
- (decision by coach) Custom Down Payment Financing is the mixed scenario; the POC reference table now shows all three options, financing cost, generated savings return, return difference versus cash, and terminal savings balance.
- (technique by coach) One Feature Only outcome: make the comparison of cash, mixed, and full financing cost versus retained/lost savings return exceptionally trustworthy; switched to Disney Method.
- (direction by user) The first result view must show exactly four decision outputs: total loan cost, savings returns retained or lost, final savings balance, and reserve warnings.
- (decision by coach) The POC scope file now defines the four-output primary result display for cash, mixed, and full financing.
- (technique by coach) Disney Method moved from Dreamer to Realist after defining the four-output first-screen contract.
- (idea by user) Lean POC inputs: purchase amount, loan interest rate, savings interest rate, existing savings with confirmation, loan duration when borrowing, and fixed loan fees.
- (insight by coach) To calculate mixed financing and the four promised outputs, the lean form also needs Down Payment, Monthly Available Amount, and Emergency Reserve; Terminal Residual Value is not needed for the first-screen outputs and may be deferred.
- (direction by user) Savings return assumptions must be set per savings asset because Strata can contain distinct products such as PEE, Livret A, LEP, and Assurance Vie.
- (question by coach) A purchase or Down Payment changes several savings balances; the POC needs a deterministic withdrawal rule, with proportional withdrawal by current balance recommended over user-selected ordering.
- (insight by user) Different savings balances and rates mean retaining one asset rather than another changes the Cash Financing result; source-of-funds allocation is a real product decision.
- (insight by coach) The POC must expose an explicit withdrawal allocation rule and must not silently optimize account selection; proportional withdrawal is a deterministic candidate, not yet accepted.
- (direction by user) Savings assets can be blocked or unavailable for financing; not every asset balance belongs in the available savings pool.
- (decision by coach) POC now requires explicit per-asset availability confirmation. Blocked assets are excluded from Starting Savings and proportional withdrawal, remain untouched, and asset names do not encode legal or tax availability.
- (decision by user) The POC shows all savings assets with an availability toggle; the user confirms which assets can fund the scenario before calculation.
- (technique by coach) Disney Method moved from Realist to Critic after defining explicit per-asset availability confirmation.
- (insight by coach) Primary critic risk: Cheapest Financing can appear to be a universal recommendation even though results depend on eligible assets, individual returns, withdrawal allocation, reserve, monthly availability, and loan assumptions.
- (decision by coach) POC results must expose material assumptions and keep Cheapest Financing separate from Savings Return and Final Scenario Savings Balance.
- (direction by user) Converge now: ship the POC, then iterate toward greater financing-scenario complexity, precision, and accuracy.
- (technique by coach) Started convergence with MoSCoW to separate the shippable POC from later financing precision.
- (direction by user) The POC must support multi-year loans such as 60 months: calculate monthly, show yearly checkpoints, and retain monthly detail for inspection and exact reserve-breach timing.
- (decision by coach) Timeline is promoted into the POC: monthly calculation and reserve detection, yearly summary display, and monthly detail on demand.
- (decision by user) Release the financing-scenarios POC first, then iterate toward greater complexity, precision, and accuracy.
- (decision by coach) Final POC boundary: monthly calculations with yearly checkpoints and monthly detail; per-asset availability and returns; proportional withdrawal among eligible assets; three financing options; four primary outputs; Terminal Residual Value and overall Scenario Position deferred.
